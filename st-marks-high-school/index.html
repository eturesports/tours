Cultural Program

Cultural Program

Spain Beyond the Pitch

Barcelona

Sagrada Família

Guided visit to Gaudí’s basilica, the symbol of Barcelona and the city’s most visited monument.

Barcelona

Mercat de la Boqueria

The market off La Rambla — a walk through Catalan food culture, right in the historic centre.

Valencia

Ciutat de les Arts i les Ciències

Calatrava’s City of Arts and Sciences — Valencia’s landmark futuristic complex and the largest aquarium in Europe.

Valencia

La Albufera

Boat trip on the freshwater lagoon and rice fields south of Valencia — the birthplace of paella, best at sunset.

Peñíscola

Castillo de Peñíscola

The Templar sea castle where Benedict XIII — Papa Luna — held the papacy until 1423, above the medieval old town.

Valencia

LaLiga Stadiums & Live Game

Behind-the-scenes stadium tour — dressing rooms, pitch side, trophy room — plus a live LaLiga match, subject to fixtures.

All cultural activities are coordinated by the ETURE team and included in the programme budget, open to the full travelling party of players and coaching staff. This list is a starting point — we are open to suggestions, so tell us what your group would like to see and we will build it into the itinerary.
